Our Mission Statement

We stire to build clean, creative, and accessible software, while adhering to the various processes of the software development life cyle. We value curiosity and honest communication, and leaarning from each other. Our goal is to turn ideas into projects while supporting every teammates along the way.

Team Rules

  • Treat every team member and their opinions with respec
  • Be open to (constructive) criticis
  • Important decisions are put up to a vote with majority rule
  • Communicate if you are unable to attend a team meeting
  • Each member must put in the same amount of effort as the rest of the group while working on assignments
  • Focus only on the work and features due for the current Sprint, don’t get sidetracked by other features
